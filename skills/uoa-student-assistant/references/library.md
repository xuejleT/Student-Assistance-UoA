# Library Catalogue and Talis

Start from the University Library homepage or the matching Canvas course. The official borrowing page has links to **Search the Catalogue** and **My Library Account**. The November 2026 Library Search refresh can move search, request and account controls. Reopen from a current Library page and use the visible interface. [University Library](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/library.html) · [Borrowing](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/library/use-our-collections/borrowing.html) · [Library Catalogue refresh](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/news/2026/10/26/library-catalogue-refresh.html)

## Assigned readings

1. Match the Canvas course code, year and teaching period. Open **Reading Lists** or an observed week-specific reading link in **Modules**. If the tab is absent, inspect the relevant Module, course instructions or Syllabus. For a publicly visible list, use the University's [Talis search](https://auckland.rl.talis.com/) and verify its teaching period and relationship to the course. Search the catalogue by a course citation if the list is unavailable. [University student guidance](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/students/academic-information/exams-and-final-results/online-exams/exams-for-students-studying-remotely/online-learning.html)
2. Check the list heading, module or week and the item's prescribed or optional status. Capture the author, title, edition/year, chapter or pages and DOI if shown. If the current list explicitly assigns the same work as another source, link them and keep the course-specific context. Keep the assigned edition and pages distinct from other editions and general recommendations.
3. Follow the visible online-resource or holdings link. If prompted, use the observed official university or Library sign-in. Independently verify the destination after any handoff. Confirm the assigned chapter, pages or article actually open before summarizing its contents. Record which part was read when the viewer provides only a preview. [Connection workflow](connection.md)
4. Return a reading index grouped by teaching week or theme with stable Canvas/Talis, catalogue or DOI links. Mark each entry as citation only, print holding, preview, login required, blocked, or full text verified as applicable. Keep the last check time and concrete next step for unresolved items. Course reading lists are evidence of what is assigned. Search results are suggestions until matched to a course source. [University Talis guidance](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/staff/education-office/learning-teaching-policies/copyright-at-auckland/teaching/talis.html)

## Search and access

- For a known item start with title plus author, DOI or ISBN, then check the matching edition or journal details. For discovery use topic terms and visible scope or filters for format, date, subject and availability. Open the record to verify the match. Treat a catalogue entry, publisher abstract and readable full text as separate states. [Library Catalogue guide](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/assets/library/documents/introduction-to-the-catalogue-nov2022.pdf)
- For previous exams, follow the official collection entry and verify the course, semester, year and actual PDF separately. [Past exam papers workflow](past-papers.md)
- If the requested material is unavailable, check an official open version, another location or another edition and label the differences. Report whether the search ran successfully, a holding was unavailable or online access needs sign-in. Describe official request options when relevant. Keep licensed material in the requested private workspace and share citations or excerpts within applicable access and copyright limits. Use stable item links in reports and keep proxy session URLs, account identifiers and private records out of shared outputs.

## Loans, holds and requests

- For read-only status, verify **My Library Account** and inspect the named items, request status and effective return or collection dates. A recall can change an earlier due date. Standard items may auto-renew subject to conditions. Use the actual account date and status for planning. [Library borrowing](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/library/use-our-collections/borrowing.html)
- Renew, request, cancel or send a scan/interlibrary request only when the user has authorized that action. Resolve the title/edition, target copy, pickup library or chapter/pages as appropriate, and any fee or terms shown before sending. Then verify the item-specific request status, new due date or rejection in the account. If a request times out, inspect the account for it before retrying. Distinguish a placed request from an item ready for pickup and a requested scan from a delivered copy. [Click and Collect](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/library/use-our-collections/click-and-collect.html)
